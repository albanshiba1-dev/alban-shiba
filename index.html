ABOUT ALBAN

Alban Shiba is an Albanian goalkeeper from Tirana with a sporting background covering multiple football formats and competitive environments.

His experience includes futsal, football, 5x5, 9x9 and beach soccer, with appearances for Albanian clubs, the Albania Futsal National Team and international competitions.
