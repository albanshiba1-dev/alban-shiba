ABOUT ALBAN

Alban Shiba is an Albanian goalkeeper from Tirana with experience across multiple football formats, including futsal, football, 5x5, 9x9 and beach soccer.

His career includes appearances in Albanian futsal competitions, international tournaments and national-team environments.

Official competition records published by the Albanian Football Federation (FSHF) document Alban Shiba as a goalkeeper in Albanian competitions.
