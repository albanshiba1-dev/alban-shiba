PLAYER PROFILE

Alban Shiba is an Albanian goalkeeper from Tirana with sporting experience across multiple football formats.

His career includes experience with Albanian futsal, football, 5x5 and 9x9 teams, as well as participation in national and international competitions.

His sporting experience also includes international futsal activities with Albania, UEFA competition environments, PCU Games Belgium, Socca World Cup and Beach Soccer.
